The rejected screenshots show why architecture must follow the revised playable contacts and stay out of the lower-route/headroom and moving envelopes.

`EnvironmentPlan.Get(layout)` supplies one low octagonal terrain base, a raised keep base, eight small rock/fern pockets, four cypresses and four mounted outer lanterns. All are noncolliding and below the first route. Four low outer lights are the maximum additional lights; the lead should retain the existing five useful course guidance lights and existing moving lantern cores. No particle emitter, transparent foliage, extra background buildings or giant mountains.

It preserves both existing helper files. Run with a fresh project output directory. This helper has not run Blender or uploaded anything.

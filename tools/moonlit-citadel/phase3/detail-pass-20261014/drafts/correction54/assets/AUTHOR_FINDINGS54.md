# Focused author findings

I inspected the current Source53 native originals: gate night 01, west climb night 02, gate day 07, and machinery night 05. These are visual observations from the saved originals, not a final reviewer verdict.

The gate's small details do not divide the large lower wall fields into a convincing structural rhythm. The west climb has the same scale mismatch: the wall and roof masses dominate, while the small relief and roof pieces read as isolated marks. Existing Niche and QuoinCornice geometry can address those fields when grouped and seated at the actual wall scale; another masonry kit would add cost without solving placement.

Planting needs a physically supported close layer. Placement measurements show the old gate shrubs about 90 studs below the route, so making those ground props denser would not fill the camera view. The new 24-wide wall garden uses a continuous stone bowl and three visible sloped corbels, a convex earth pocket, three broad-leaf groups, and hanging vines. Its backing intersects the old wall by 0.10 stud. Gate base Y -2 puts the highest leaves at Y 7.628, close to the route camera at Y 5.5. Reduced copies fit the measured west wall jambs and the machinery wall below its warm windows.

The machinery original does not show the yard winches at route height. Rotating the whole existing ground asset onto a gable would also rotate its crate and loose tools, so that transform needs rejection. A reduced wall hoist containing only the existing timbers, drum, rope, and handwheel would be coherent if separately authorized; the completed DG01 garden payload is frozen independently.

The three new original Blender kit images show supported planting and clean contours. All three closed material meshes and the actual FBX vertex roundtrip pass. Final map composition, native importer basis, placement clearance, and owner acceptance are still for the lead's native pass. No Source52 geometry or accepted course feature was edited by this author.

# Course ImageGen recipes — V3

October 9, 2026. Use the [three-phase workflow](COURSE_WORKFLOW_V3.md) and [Ideation guide](COURSE_IDEATION_WORKFLOW.md). Start from the Phase 0 blueprint. Develop one phase at a time: one first reference, owner amendments, then its selected supporting images before starting the next phase. Phase 1 has 2–10 selected images; Phases 2 and 3 have 4–6 each, including the refined first reference. Extra appearance or clay studies are optional for a named design question.

These are writing recipes for Course Ideation. Fill every bracketed field from the current brief, remove irrelevant fields, and save fully expanded, ready-to-paste prompts in the course's `VISUAL_INSPIRATION_PROMPTS.md`. The owner should not need to combine fragments or fill fields. Use natural language for ChatGPT ImageGen, without guessed model names, Midjourney flags or API-only options. Each generation requests one readable image.

## Shared facts and inputs

Record the course name, brief/layout/visual revision, intended form and height, orientation, ordered sections, connections, branches, checkpoints, hazards, landmarks, avatar scale, palette, material language and three visible qualities to preserve. Use only the facts relevant to each image. The course form determines its footprint; camera framing adapts to it. A square garden example does not require a square base, perimeter fence, garden theme or rectangular districts in another course.

Name inputs by number and role: native-plan preview for route connections, selected phase master as an edit target, or appearance example for style. Do not assume every attachment is an edit target. When a phase is derived from another phase's concept, identify exactly what to add, retain or simplify. Keep the same course rather than generating unrelated designs for different stages.

Default presentation: an isometric-style 3D showcase with an orthographic camera, quiet background and framing that reveals the intended course. Specify camera A, world orientation and useful scale once; reuse them across phases where practical. Phase 1 may need framing suited to its asset kit and sample. Keep lighting/scale comparable and record intentional differences.

Use the accepted appearance direction, including its stud treatment. The standing quality target is polished stylized detail: substantial silhouettes, selective curved edges/trim, natural layering and readable material variation, with no imaginary polygon budget inferred from the image. Phase 1's finished sample already demonstrates the Phase 3 finish quality; rough areas are deliberately marked by simpler treatment.

## 1. Phase 1 master — Asset Creation

```text
Create one [aspect ratio suited to the course] isometric-style 3D showcase for [course name], showing the intended END OF PHASE 1: ASSET CREATION.

Inputs: Image 1 is [native plan preview], the authority for the course form, section order and connections. Image 2 is [appearance reference, if present], used for [specific accepted visual qualities]. [Identify any existing phase image and its exact role.]

Show [important reusable asset families] together with [representative playable section], finished enough to judge shape, detail, normal-avatar scale and how the pieces fit. Keep the wider course as a rough outline of [course form and major height relationships]. Clearly distinguish any displayed kit pieces from objects installed on the route. Preserve [three visual anchors] and the sample's intended [normal action, expert contrast if present, warning and recovery space].

Camera A: [world-facing direction, elevation, framing and scale]. Use orthographic projection and an isometric-style presentation. Adapt the image bounds to [actual footprint]; preserve the intended heights and proportions. Use [materials, palette and lighting] with a quiet [background].

Keep [specific connections, landmarks, asset identity and preserved details]. Leave [later-stage surroundings] rough. No software interface, HUD, invented labels or decorative text. This is an appearance and composition target; do not imply measured dimensions, a complete production kit or verified gameplay.
```

The finished sample and rough route are within Phase 1. Do not make this image imply that the whole course has already been assembled. Actual kit completeness, imports, editability and contact are Creator checks.

## 2. Phase 2 master — Course Creation

```text
Create one [aspect ratio] isometric-style orthographic showcase for [course name], showing the intended END OF PHASE 2: COURSE CREATION.

Inputs: Image 1 is [current native plan preview] and defines the route. Image 2 is [selected Phase 1 master or accepted appearance image] and defines the asset/style language. [If editing an existing Phase 2 image, identify it as the edit target.]

Show the entire connected playable course in [accepted course form]. Preserve the ordered journey [concise section sequence], [branches and rejoins], [checkpoints], [signature obstacles and warnings], and [failure/recovery/passing spaces]. Use the finished garden/architectural/natural objects themselves to communicate [specific movement actions]. Keep rough masses for [large surrounding forms] so enclosure, camera space and sightlines are visible; retain the selected quality on playable objects.

Use camera A [matching position, orientation, elevation and scale] with orthographic projection and [consistent lighting]. Keep [landmarks, major height relationships and three visual anchors] in their agreed locations. Adapt the frame to the route's footprint; preserve clear landings and hazard information.

Leave [Phase 3 surrounding detail and atmosphere work] visibly unfinished. Preserve [fixed details]. No software interface, HUD, invented connections, decorative text or planning labels unless specifically listed. The image describes the target course; it does not prove that its jumps, hazards, capacity or timing work.
```

Major surroundings belong in the composition early, even when their detailed artwork is unfinished.

## 3. Phase 3 master — Map Creation

```text
Create one [aspect ratio] isometric-style orthographic showcase for [course name], showing the intended END OF PHASE 3: MAP CREATION.

Inputs: Image 1 is [selected Phase 2 master], the course composition to preserve. Image 2 is [current native plan preview], the authority for route connections. [Other inputs and precise appearance-only roles, if needed.]

Complete the surrounding world with [near-route layers], [middle-distance section identity], [distant context], [transitions], [atmosphere] and [earned destination]. Show [three visual qualities to preserve] through concrete shapes, materials and planted/architectural layers. Keep normal-avatar scale and enough openings to read landings, hazards, route choices and recovery.

Retain [ordered sections, branches, checkpoints, obstacle locations, major heights and landmark positions]. Make the surroundings support this course without moving a connection or concealing an important warning. Any requested route redesign must already be reflected in the attached plan.

Use camera A [matching position, orientation, elevation and scale], orthographic projection and [accepted lighting/atmosphere]. Preserve [course footprint and height character]; fit the frame to it. No forced square display base, new districts, HUD, software interface or decorative text. This is the finished appearance target, with actual gameplay and resource checks still required in Roblox.
```

## Iterate before adding angles

Use one targeted edit at a time. Restate important accepted details and attach the selected master as the edit target. Propagate a changed layout or style into the brief, diagram and other affected phase masters before generating their remaining views.

```text
Edit Image 1, the selected [phase] master for [course and revision].
Change only [specific requested adjustment and its intended visible result].
Keep [route connections, landmarks, proportions, palette, asset identity, camera and all unaffected details] unchanged.
Image 2 is [supporting input] and controls only [its exact role].
Preserve the phase boundary: [what is already finished and what remains rough].
Return one readable image with the same framing and no added labels or interface.
```

Keep one selected master per phase. Mark superseded images as history and clearly identify proposals versus owner-selected directions. Do not treat a prompt request to preserve geometry as proof that it was preserved.

## 4. Supporting references — expand the reviewed current phase

The selected initial image normally serves as reference A and counts toward the total. Expand the reviewed current phase to 2–10 images for Phase 1 or 4–6 for Phase 2/3. Name each image's job before generating it: asset family, assembly, operating position, player-height sample, overview, reverse or cutaway. Choose the count from useful coverage, not a maximum quota. Reuse corresponding cameras across later phases when helpful. For an asset or assembly study, adapt the camera-only recipe below to its exact subject while preserving the accepted design. Record purpose and framing in the index.

```text
Create one additional orthographic 3D showcase view of the SAME [course], [phase], [design revision].

Inputs: Image 1 is the selected phase master and defines the accepted design, asset identity, materials and phase completion state. Image 2 is [native plan preview] and defines section order, connections, branches, major heights and landmark positions. [Other input only if it resolves a specific hidden area.]

Reference [ID], purpose [specific question or coverage]: camera at [named world direction and elevation], aimed toward [known course anchor or asset assembly], with [framing and scale]. Keep [agreed lighting and world orientation]. Change only the stated viewpoint or subject framing. [If needed, describe the exact cutaway or operating position without inventing geometry.]

Preserve [ordered sections and connections], [landmark positions], [relative heights], [asset families and scale], [palette], [warnings/recovery spaces] and [finished versus unfinished phase content]. Do not mirror or rearrange the course to improve composition. For hidden relationships use the supplied plan; do not invent a bridge, branch, hazard, checkpoint or destination.

Return one full readable image, not a multi-panel sheet, with no software interface, HUD or new labels.
```

ImageGen views require inspection for layout and appearance consistency. When exact matching geometry is required, render the necessary angles from one shared 3D scene with geometry, materials and world lighting fixed. Reuse a suitable scene when available; a substantial reconstruction is separate work, not the default Ideation prerequisite. Label reconstruction renders honestly and keep the selected ImageGen appearance target alongside them. Rebuilding a view in 3D does not prove it faithfully reproduces every concept detail.

## Optional supporting images

Create an extra mood board or clay study only when it answers a named unresolved question. Useful player-height, material, asset or section studies can form part of the current phase's selected set when their purpose is explicit. Use the same plan/style authority and inspect the result. Do not automatically add a separate mandatory package. Actual Creator screenshots at player height remain necessary regardless of concept-image count.

## Check before creator startup

- Confirm three phase targets and the required selected references: 2–10 for Phase 1 and 4–6 each for Phases 2 and 3, including each selected first image, unless the owner specified an exception or partial scope. Each image must have a clear purpose.
- Inspect every selected image against the brief and native plan for course form, orientation, section order, route/branch connections, landmark positions, heights, checkpoint/hazard locations and avatar scale.
- Check each phase's finished/unfinished content and the sample's relationship to the final quality target.
- Check cross-view appearance and geometry; correct or reject contradictions rather than combining incompatible pictures.
- Keep the image index in `VISUAL_INSPIRATION_PROMPTS.md`: phase, camera/view, filename, source type, selected master, revision compatibility, accepted/proposed status and unresolved discrepancies. Resolve material construction conflicts before marking the package ready.
- Preserve exact local project paths and label ImageGen concepts, shared-scene reconstructions and Studio evidence separately. Keep prompts-only or partial deliveries explicit.
- Record the phase completion criteria, preserved design choices and Creator's allowed flexibility in the brief/starter. Images do not establish playability, performance or owner acceptance of an unseen build.

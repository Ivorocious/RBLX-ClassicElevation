# Race flow and visit practice

Current implementation: [race-flow task](artifacts/game-flow-study-2026-10-02/CURRENT_STATE.md). Native and human evidence are recorded separately there.

Players explicitly choose Start racing or Join this/next race. Enrollment lasts until Take a break; arrival does not enroll. Entry closes before the final three-second countdown after current-character, delivered-floor, loaded-course and restored-camera checks. A changed or late character gets the next-race outcome. Official finishes and unfinished progress remain intact when activity changes.

Choose one opening course. A solo accepted choice starts preparing promptly; multiplayer voting lasts 15 seconds. Five eligible courses produce a five-course event without repeats. Fewer produce an ordinary race. The owner removed Classic Circuit and Classic Point-to-Point on October 14. Their native models are recovered and removed, and source blocks their discovery, selection and automatic recreation. The current eligible catalog has City Run, Keyboard Circuit, Moonlit Citadel and Shipwreck Cove; Stud Garden's existing Studio inspection access does not change race eligibility.

Intermediate races target results at T+0–2, loading at T+2, preview at T+8–11, then three seconds with normal control and a start around T+14. Slow delivery holds before countdown and ends with a useful retry/lobby outcome after bounded recovery. Skip cinematic and Reduce motion are separate. Grand Arcade shows actual course history, winners/times and remaining official racers; the finale is optional and adds no overall champion scoring.

Practice is reached through a portal; it is a separate area in the same place. The lobby portal is at `(1552,392,-836)`. `Workspace.VisitPracticeAreaC01` is centered at `(0,1000,-6000)` with its own floor, Normal and Hard circuits and a return portal. Normal copies the eight accepted lobby pad shapes without changing those originals. Each line has 12 physical pads, two laps, 23 ordered checkpoint touches and a final return to Start / Finish. A small persistent model keeps this area available under instance streaming. [Roblox streaming guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/streaming).

Enter practice portal is also available in Race options while safely in the lobby. Enter does not begin the clock; choose Normal or Hard. Actual finish holds its result until a choice, and Retry begins a new attempt. Return to lobby works through the return portal or Race options. Practice has separate visit bests and no official placement, reward or DNF. Enrolled players receive a departure notice and are moved for their next race; players taking a break remain in the area.

Visit progress compares compatible real course/revision/route/format/lap identities. It counts only races entered, valid finishes, best times/placements, supported falls/checkpoints and real improvements. It survives event resets but not disconnect/rejoin. Expert targets require verified course support. This task adds no persistent progression, currency rule, purchase or profile-schema change.

Tuning lives in `RaceConfig`, `GrandArcadeConfig`, `TournamentConfig` and `PracticeConfig`. Inactivity warns at 120 seconds and moves to break at 150 seconds; movement and legitimate attempts reset it. Course deadlines remain conservative authored assumptions until beginner sessions measure them. Practice target duration is 20–40 seconds; two-lap tuning is based on controlled physics evidence, and human timing/fun remains pending.

For local testing, finish arrival, explicitly enroll, open F6 and use **Start event (uses enrolled players)** to release the Studio held lobby. Existing Start race/map/checkpoint/unlimited-time inspection controls remain. Use the current actual catalog and choose a finished course. Local-server fake user IDs cannot fetch real account avatars; the existing Returning racer preset supplies temporary Custom appearance for those test clients.

The owner will author the practice course later. Keep the current portal/starter area basic; further practice design, polish and human timing acceptance are deferred.

Watching requests one server-approved streaming focus for the selected official racer. The client waits up to six seconds for that body before restoring the lobby camera; next/previous selection replaces the focus, and exit or target loss removes it. [Roblox replication-focus guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/streaming#replication-focus).

See the [long-session plan](artifacts/game-flow-study-2026-10-02/LONG_SESSION_TEST_PLAN.md) for the human acceptance gate. Whole-place save and publishing are separate owner actions.

## Screens (UI overhaul, October 2026)

The race-flow screens keep their actions, button order, camera leases and the course delivery handshake; only their looks changed. `RaceFlowView` draws Race options as a slide-up Sky Rush ticket (first button large, the rest in a two-column grid, same keyboard/controller navigation and 6-second pending timeout). `VisitCoachView` replaces it while Show me around runs (three step bars, Skip introduction, and Start racing on the last step, which sends the existing `Join`). `PracticeView` shows the line choice, the running HUD (timer, pad strip and lap from the snapshot's `padCount`/`lapCount`/`nextCheckpoint`, best this visit, Retry, LAP ribbon, pink departure countdown) and the finish burst; entering the practice area now opens the line choice. `GalleryVotingView` is the one-course vote. `NextCourseView` is the next-course ticket; `TournamentPresentation` calls it through a guard so a UI error can never break delivery, and a failed preparation shows a pink ticket with the server's message. `WatchView` (through the race HUD) is the Watching screen; `ResultsScreen` replaces `RaceResultsUI` with a 3D-avatar podium held through loading; `EventEndView` is the event end with the optional finale. `GrandArcadeService` restyles the arch screens (looks only).

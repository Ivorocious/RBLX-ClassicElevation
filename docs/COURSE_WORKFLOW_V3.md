# Course workflow V3

October 20, 2026. Standing workflow for **Course Ideation** and focused **Course Creator — course** chats. The owner's latest request and applicable AGENTS.md govern. The owner should not need to repeat the workflow or reconstruct a creator prompt.

**Better process means better output and faster work.** Quality takes priority within the commissioned scope. Reduce avoidable reinterpretation, rebuilding, repeated checking and waiting; do not substitute more agents, documents or passing scores for a stronger course.

## The production sequence

Keep **Phase 0 — Course Blueprint** as planning, followed by three production phases:

| Phase | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| **1 — Asset Creation** | A majority reusable kit, a cheap whole-route study and one finished representative playable section. Include ordinary obstacles and a branch where relevant, alongside the signature interaction and intended art. |
| **2 — Course Creation** | The complete playable journey: varied movement, meaningful choices, ordered progress, fair hazards, recovery and room for compact racing. Rough large surroundings expose space and camera problems early. |
| **3 — Map Creation** | A complete surrounding world with close composition, section identity, atmosphere, transitions and a rewarding destination; affected gameplay remains correct. |

Phase 0 establishes the journey, connections/heights, movement choices, recovery, provisional timing and asset needs in the brief and editable draw.io plan. It is not a Studio build. A plan cannot prove movement, timing or capacity.

Reference development remains sequential: **Phase 1 first image → owner amendments → Phase 1 selected set**, then the same process for Phases 2 and 3. Phase 1 has **2–10 selected references**; Phases 2 and 3 have **4–6 each**, including the refined first image. Maximum counts are not quotas. Follow the [Ideation guide](COURSE_IDEATION_WORKFLOW.md) and [image recipes](COURSE_IMAGEGEN_PROMPTS.md); respect partial commissions and explicit owner exceptions. Do not impose the superseded three-masters-first/twelve-image package.

Once the course build is commissioned, Ideation creates its fresh focused creator using the [starter](FOCUSED_CREATOR_THREADS.md) and verifies startup. A proposal or reference review alone does not authorize construction.

The rough playable journey and finished section belong inside Phase 1 and the existing owner critique checkpoint. Present them together before broad expansion unless that checkpoint is satisfied or explicitly waived. Do not add a separate approval for each asset or check. Continue through already authorized phases once their real checkpoints are met.

## What defines success

The brief states the required player experience; native draw.io defines planned connections/order; selected images define appearance. Current owner corrections override older sources. Reconcile material conflicts before expensive work, and record intentional tested layout changes. Concepts do not establish exact dimensions or permit accidental checkpoint/hazard bypasses.

Carry these priorities into each course:

- **Playable variety:** every section has an ordinary action sequence, a purpose and a memorable moment. Signature mechanisms do not replace their surrounding course.
- **Meaningful choices:** Normal and Expert have different demands and useful rewards between common fork/rejoin points; preserve the required encounters and recovery.
- **Finished appearance:** polished stylized forms, connected surroundings at player height, readable landings/hazards, distinct local settings and an earned destination. Choose the footprint and stud treatment from this course, not an earlier theme.
- **Practical performance:** optimize early while preserving those qualities. Counts and simulation are not measured device/full-race performance.
- **Useful editability:** preserve owner work and keep functional units understandable. Respect a task-specific waiver of easy manual editing while retaining durable source and recovery.

**Blender is the main authoring tool for custom visible course assets and meaningful geometry.** Reuse suitable existing or inspected Toolbox assets. Native Studio handles assembly, intentional contacts, gameplay and rough studies; other visible construction methods need a clear quality, editability or runtime-cost benefit. Use the [Blender workflow](BLENDER_BUILD_WORKFLOW.md) for substantial authored work.

The standing race targets are **20 racers**, compact competitive spacing, **4–6 minutes for average players** and **2–3 minutes for practiced experts**, unless the owner changes them. Course format and lap count remain specific. Separate clean travel, forced waiting, hesitation and recovery. Resolve a large early pacing mismatch through meaningful movement design, not empty distance, artificial pauses or untested traffic allowances.

## One current course record

Use one existing brief/status record with the [shared fields](FOCUSED_CREATOR_THREADS.md#compact-current-state-template). Keep at its top:

```text
Current phase/candidate and latest owner instruction:
Desired player-visible result; accepted examples and traits:
What changes; what stays; previously rejected approaches:
Required actions, branches, encounters and timing assumptions:
Current references, source/native target and recovery:
Permissions, helper/reviewer settings and in-flight work:
Completed proof, remaining defect/question and next action:
Owner acceptance/closure and any historical evidence limits:
```

Keep this record editable. Freeze a snapshot for a review when needed; do not freeze the live status and then accumulate contradictory “current” headers around it. Link earlier reports as history. On resume, compare current instructions with relevant files/live facts before mutations or interruptions. A closed course cannot become the next task through an old summary.

## Make each correction count

Translate feedback into a specific expected change and preservation boundary before implementation. For example, improve the usefulness of arrows without assuming more arrows, or add a depth veil while retaining the floor. Check the latest accepted/rejected examples. A request to improve one trait does not reject the rest of the design.

For a costly uncertain choice, compare a few cheap local alternatives while keeping the rest constant. Routine judgment belongs to the lead; ask only for a material unresolved decision. Do not generate several complete packages or rebuild an accepted map to settle one local question.

After repeated qualitative feedback, explain why the earlier approach failed and change its cause. Fix composition, challenge or the diagnostic method. Repeating small detail changes or another score round is not evidence of improvement.

## Verification and independent review

The [Creator guide](COURSE_CREATOR_WORKFLOW.md#proportional-verification-and-evidence) is the single operating checklist for build checks, review and tools. Commission only the required phase evidence; do not copy every possible test into the starter.

The lead checks the whole experience before final review. Reviewers judge the brief and actual installed result, challenge important assumptions and retain independent conclusions. Source identity and technical correctness support that judgment. They do not close missing core difficulty, navigation or appearance.

Preserve the owner's requested reviewer count, roles, models and threshold. Reuse the same judges for affected corrections with clear candidate identity. No averaging, score pressure, inherited old scores or unrequested extra review layer. Separate required missing proof from optional future work. Owner acceptance can close the job without turning unperformed tests into passes.

## Scope, tools and delivery

State the current objective, target, outputs and completion condition once. Use already granted operations and destinations, including covered routine revisions/uploads. A new filename, failed check or “continue” does not reset authority. Prepare concrete work before asking for genuinely missing permission.

General course authority does not cover publishing, purchases, account/toolchain changes, unrelated deletion or economy/DataStore/security redesign. Preserve manual edits and recoverable native/source work. Follow an actual automatic-review rejection; do not bypass it through another tool.

One lead controls the intended Studio and test session. Authorized helpers prepare independent outputs with explicit model/effort and ownership. Use short relevant context and compact results. Model defaults remain in the [focused starter](FOCUSED_CREATOR_THREADS.md#creator-model-defaults); this reset changes no app setting or active-job selection.

At delivery, show the installed result, important changes, actual checks, limits and recovery. State how to test it in Studio and separate Save/publication status. Stop at the commissioned boundary or continue into an already authorized phase. Keep completed courses accepted and closed.

## Assess the result

In the existing record, keep a short phase summary: missed-requirement corrections, repeated defects, genuine new scope, recorded elapsed time and known tool/approval waits. Do not invent active time, add overlapping helper time or treat raw cached-token counts as billed usage. Compare first-pass output and remaining owner repair with the accepted sample and target.

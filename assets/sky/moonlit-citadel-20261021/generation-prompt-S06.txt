Idea F: PAINTED MIDNIGHT. A deliberately art-directed, hand-painted storybook game sky rather than photographic clouds. A rich desaturated indigo-blue night with broad, simple sculpted cloud shapes in layered slate-blue and muted silver-blue. Cloud edges are clear but soft, with graceful curved silhouettes and large quiet color planes. Think an accomplished environment painter simplifying atmospheric forms for a stylized block-built castle. Subtle painterly gradients within shapes; no noisy brush texture, grain, fine fractal wisps or realistic thunderheads. Keep the middle upper sky spacious and mostly clear. Several long layered cloud shapes drift horizontally near the horizon with one or two smaller higher shapes; clouds form a calm frame around the open sky. A very sparse scattering of tiny star points, no giant sparkles. Slightly warmer smoky gray cloud edges may complement amber windows, but the sky remains cool midnight blue. Charming, composed and quietly mysterious, not childish or cartoon sticker clouds. No neon color, galaxy, aurora, nebula, lightning or storm.
True full-sphere equirectangular sky texture, horizon exactly at horizontal center; top edge zenith, bottom edge nadir. Seamlessly wrap left/right edges. Both pole edges nearly uniform. Lower hemisphere is simple dark blue-gray atmospheric color with faint layers dissolving into a uniform nadir; no landscape or scenery. No moon or sun: Roblox will display one moon separately. No ground, mountains, trees, ocean, buildings or skyline. No text, labels, borders, watermark, grid, panels, cube cross or mockup. The entire image is one continuous environment texture.

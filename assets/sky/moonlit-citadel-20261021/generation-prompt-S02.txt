Idea B: STILL STARLIGHT. A spacious, beautifully quiet, clear midnight sky. Desaturated ink-indigo and slate-blue, a smooth gentle gradient from deep midnight blue overhead to muted smoky blue-gray at the horizon. Just a few very soft thin horizontal low cloud wisps, kept far below the upper two thirds. Most of the sky is open, calm and still. Add a restrained scattering of small distant stars only across the upper hemisphere: fine naturally varied pinpoints with broad empty spaces, subtle enough to support a castle composition. No brilliant starbursts, no Milky Way band, no galaxy, nebula, aurora or colorful cosmic texture. Sophisticated painterly game-sky finish, clean gradients, subtle atmospheric depth. Quiet, slightly mysterious, never pitch black or gray flatness. No cloud drama or bright horizon glow.
Full-sphere texture projection, not a landscape illustration: horizon exactly at the image horizontal center, top edge zenith, bottom edge nadir. Left and right edges wrap seamlessly. Both pole edges nearly uniform, stars stay away from the top edge. Below the horizon is only soft blue-gray atmosphere fading to a uniform dark slate-blue at nadir; no ground, mountains, ocean, trees, architecture, silhouettes or horizon line. No moon or sun in the art; Roblox adds the one intended moon separately. No text, watermark, labels, frame, grid, cubemap cross or mockup. Sky only, full canvas.
